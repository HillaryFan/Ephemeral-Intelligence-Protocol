# Ephemeral Intelligence Protocol™ (EIP™)

## From governed human-AI work to sustainable enterprise capability

> An original, validation-ready enterprise AI operating-model framework developed from pharmaceutical R&D, Clinical Development, and Clinical Operations. Prepared for bounded organizational pilot testing and refinement.

Enterprise AI can generate useful outputs faster than most organizations can govern, validate, and learn from them. The harder problem is organizational: deciding what AI-enabled work may be trusted, reused, scaled, changed, or retired - and who retains the authority to decide.

The Ephemeral Intelligence Protocol™ (EIP™) addresses that operating-model gap. It provides a governed path for converting a small, selected portion of human-AI work into reusable enterprise capability without treating every prompt, transcript, or model output as institutional knowledge.

## Governing principle

**Default ephemeral. Retain and institutionalize only what has been validated, governed, and shown to add enterprise value.**

EIP separates temporary exploration from required evidence and from approved reusable capability. Persistence is earned through purpose, evidence, accountable ownership, defined scope, rights, monitoring, change control, and a retirement path.

## Version 2.1 public research baseline

Version 2.1 extends how EIP frames capability formation, human oversight, evidence, leadership responsibility, and human benefit. It preserves the five-protocol core, four operating layers, three interlocking loops, two entry points, and selective capability-promotion lifecycle.

The reviewed integration asks whether governed learning can reduce repeated human reconstruction while maintaining quality, legitimate authority, recovery, and appropriate escalation. It treats this as a proposition for bounded testing, not as evidence that an agent has become independently capable.

The principal v2.1 additions are:

- **Scaffold-to-Persistence** - a governed inquiry into formation, controlled withdrawal of specified cognitive support, transfer, recalibration, and restoration;
- **HITL operating range** - human authority checkpoints are distinguished from cognitive-support checkpoints, and the acceptable deployment range is treated as contextual evidence rather than a fixed staffing ratio;
- **Outcome Efficiency** - reliable productive outcomes, human cognitive sustainability, realized human benefit, and governance integrity remain separately visible;
- **New Leadership** - leaders are accountable for feedback consistency, qualified review capacity, recovery, work density, support-state decisions, and the destination of AI-released capacity;
- **Authority and competence** - permission to act and demonstrated capability are separate gates with different failure responses;
- **Thermal-Palette research direction** - experience-sensitive context modulation is retained as an optional, unvalidated research direction rather than a required EIP component.

See the [Version 2.1 public baseline](docs/V2_1_PUBLIC_BASELINE.md) for the public change summary and evidence boundary.

## What EIP is

EIP is an enterprise operating-model framework that connects:

- intended use, boundaries, decision rights, and human authority;
- frontline expertise and governed capability capture;
- selective validation, abstraction, reuse, monitoring, and retirement;
- technology, quality, privacy, security, legal, procurement, and supplier evidence;
- bounded enterprise calibration followed by a recorded decision to scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop.

## What EIP is not

EIP is not presented as:

- commercially deployed software or a mandatory middleware product;
- a substitute for accountable Quality, Regulatory, Privacy, Security, Legal, Medical, Safety, or business decisions;
- a guarantee of GxP compliance, privacy, security, regulatory acceptance, ROI, self-funding transformation, or headcount reduction;
- a requirement to retain every conversation or delete every technical record;
- an automated employee-ranking, credentialing, or talent-filter system;
- a claim that the framework has already been validated across enterprises.

## Five operating protocols

1. **AI Governance Protocol** - establishes intended use, boundaries, authority, evidence expectations, change control, and stop conditions.
2. **Frontline Capability Capture Protocol** - identifies potentially reusable expert contributions without equating AI activity with contribution quality.
3. **Capability Evolution Protocol** - tests whether a candidate lesson can become a scoped, governed, versioned, and reversible enterprise capability.
4. **Enterprise Co-Creation Protocol** - provides a controlled path for enterprise and supplier improvement when rights, evidence, responsibility, and exit are explicit.
5. **Ephemeral Intelligence Protocol** - integrates the first four protocols into one selective capability-promotion lifecycle.

The public descriptions above state the operating intent. Detailed procedures, control fields, validation tests, data structures, contract mechanisms, and implementation templates are deliberately not published in this repository.

## A bounded enterprise decision

EIP proposes a configurable 180-day Enterprise Calibration, not a clinical trial or a universal validation period. One consequential but controllable workflow is used to test the operating model against real organizational conditions. The enterprise defines the local scope, accountable owners, evidence plan, workload protections, technical and contractual boundaries, and stop conditions.

The intended output is not automatic adoption. It is an evidence-based decision to **scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop**.

## The three-volume v2.1 set

- **Volume I - Core White Paper:** strategic thesis, operating architecture, five protocols, decision rights, evidence logic, HITL operating range, formation inquiry, leadership, and human-benefit allocation.
- **Volume II - Implementation Manual:** bounded 180-day operating sequence, roles, evidence collection, workload safeguards, support-state testing, transfer, evaluation, and closure logic.
- **Volume III - Technical, Governance & Contracting Companion:** capability-first technical assurance, runtime boundaries, separate authority and competence evidence, rights, contracts, change control, handover, and exit evidence.

## Development position

This repository reflects the public-facing **v2.1 research baseline (September 2026)**. Version 2.0 remains a frozen historical baseline; Version 2.1 is a reviewed cross-volume integration draft whose selected high-level changes are summarized here.

EIP remains positioned as an original, validation-ready operating-model framework prepared for bounded organizational pilot testing and refinement. Editorial integration is not enterprise validation, regulatory acceptance, demonstrated ROI, or proof of reduced supervisory dependence. Enterprise operation is the evidence-producing next stage.
