# EIP Version 2.1 Public Baseline

## Status

Version 2.1 is the current reviewed integration draft of the Ephemeral Intelligence Protocol™. It preserves the frozen Version 2.0 source baseline and integrates selected research additions across the Core White Paper, Implementation Manual, Technical, Governance & Contracting Companion, and Executive Brief.

This is an editorial and cross-volume development milestone. It is not a claim of enterprise validation, regulatory acceptance, commercial deployment, demonstrated return on investment, or generalized agent independence. The complete volumes and internal change-control materials are not published in this repository.

## What remains unchanged

Version 2.1 does not add a sixth protocol or replace the EIP operating model. It preserves:

- Default Ephemeral and deliberate, evidence-backed persistence;
- four operating layers and cross-functional assurance;
- five operating protocols;
- three interlocking loops;
- two entry points;
- the selective capability-promotion lifecycle;
- a bounded Enterprise Calibration ending in a decision to scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop;
- human accountability, intended-use evidence, architecture neutrality, rights, change control, and exit.

## Integrated additions

| Area | Public Version 2.1 position | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scaffold-to-Persistence | Investigates whether governed assets can support reliable capability with less repeated human cognitive reconstruction. | Requires controlled withdrawal, comparison, transfer, recalibration, and restoration; no general result is claimed. |
| HITL operating range | Separates authority-preserving checkpoints from cognitive-support checkpoints and treats deployment as a contextual operating range. | No fixed human-to-agent ratio, staffing model, or declining supervision curve has been established. |
| Outcome Efficiency | Keeps reliable outcomes, human cognitive sustainability, realized human benefit, and governance integrity separately visible. | Candidate measures require local specification and validation; one aggregate score cannot hide a failed domain. |
| New Leadership | Assigns responsibility for feedback consistency, review capacity, recovery, work density, support-state decisions, and benefit allocation. | No new universal job title, trainer ratio, or incentive formula is prescribed. |
| Authority and competence | Uses separate permission and capability decisions with distinct evidence and failure responses. | Passing a competence test does not expand authority; adequate authority does not prove competence. |
| Thermal-Palette research | Retains an optional hypothesis about revisable affective and experiential estimates used with semantic, task, and outcome signals. | It is not a required EIP component and does not imply emotion, consciousness, access to true feelings, or validated performance benefit. |

## Evidence-producing next stage

A bounded organizational pilot should identify the exact intended use, configuration, support types, accountable owners, authority checkpoints, qualified review capacity, evidence plan, human-benefit objective, and stop conditions before operation.

Where support reduction is investigated, the pilot should compare matched conditions, preserve failed and contradictory evidence, test novelty and transfer, count hidden human and downstream work, and define restoration and revalidation triggers. Reduced prompting, fluent output, better recall, or self-reported confidence is insufficient evidence of reliable formation.

The result remains a governed decision: **scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop**. A support-state decision and an authority decision remain separately recorded.
