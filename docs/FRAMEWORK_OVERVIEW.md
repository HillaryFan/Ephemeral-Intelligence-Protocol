# EIP Framework Overview

## The operating-model problem

Organizations can gain local productivity from AI without building durable enterprise capability. Useful reasoning may remain trapped in private conversations, repeated by each new team, or lost when a person, model, vendor, or platform changes. At the same time, indiscriminate retention can turn drafts and unsupported outputs into apparent institutional knowledge.

EIP addresses both failures through selective, governed reuse.

## The core thesis

**Default ephemeral, deliberate persistence.**

Temporary human-AI work does not acquire institutional authority merely because it exists. A candidate outcome may become reusable only when the enterprise can state its purpose, scope, evidence, owner, applicable and prohibited contexts, rights, monitoring, change triggers, and retirement method.

The durable unit is the governed capability and its evidence - not the mere accumulation of chat history.

## Three information states

### 1. Ephemeral working state

Exploration, drafts, temporary context, alternatives, and intermediate reasoning used for the current task. This is not automatically enterprise knowledge. Technical retention and records obligations still depend on the approved environment and use.

### 2. Required evidence or record

Material retained because an audit, safety, quality, legal, security, operational, or contractual obligation requires it. A retained record is not automatically reusable guidance.

### 3. Persistent Enterprise Intelligence

An approved, evidence-backed reference blueprint, workflow, evaluation rule, controlled knowledge asset, or input to policy or standard work. It has a defined purpose, owner, scope, version, access rules, monitoring, revalidation triggers, and retirement path.

## Five protocols, one selective lifecycle

| Protocol | Public operating intent |
| --- | --- |
| AI Governance Protocol | Defines intended use, boundaries, accountable authority, evidence, change control, escalation, and stop conditions. |
| Frontline Capability Capture Protocol | Identifies potentially reusable expertise while protecting workload, fairness, attribution, correction, and appeal. |
| Capability Evolution Protocol | Tests whether a candidate lesson can be minimized, abstracted, validated, scoped, versioned, monitored, and retired. |
| Enterprise Co-Creation Protocol | Governs reciprocal improvement with suppliers when evidence, rights, responsibilities, change, portability, and exit are explicit. |
| Ephemeral Intelligence Protocol | Integrates the four protocols into a reversible path from temporary work to governed reuse. |

The detailed operating procedures and control specifications are maintained outside the public repository.

## Human authority

Human participation is not treated as a checkbox. Consequential checkpoints require sufficient context, a defined cognitive contribution, real authority to challenge or reject the output, and enough time to exercise that authority.

Version 2.1 distinguishes two functions that may coexist at a checkpoint. An **authority-preserving checkpoint** exists because permission, accountability, risk, safety, or stop rights require an authorized human decision. A **formation or cognitive-support checkpoint** supplies diagnosis, decomposition, premise repair, or next-step reasoning that a configuration may later be tested without. Evidence that supports reducing one specified cognitive support does not cancel a mandatory authority checkpoint.

EIP distinguishes AI use from contribution, a candidate signal from validation, and a validated contribution from a complete professional qualification. Any contribution indicator is bounded evidence for learning and recognition; it is not a universal employee score.

## Version 2.1 integrated research extensions

Version 2.1 adds a public research layer without changing the canonical architecture:

- a Scaffold-to-Persistence formation path that separates governed reusable assets from demonstrated operating competence;
- controlled withdrawal, transfer, novelty, interruption, escalation, and restoration questions for specified support;
- a contextual HITL operating range rather than a universal human-to-agent ratio;
- four Outcome Efficiency domains: reliable productive outcomes, human cognitive sustainability, realized human benefit, and governance integrity;
- leadership responsibility for feedback quality, review capacity, recovery, work density, benefit allocation, and support-state decisions;
- separate authority and competence records so that inadequate permission and inadequate capability trigger different responses.

The optional **Thermal-Palette** research direction considers whether revisable affective and experiential estimates could support context selection alongside semantic, task, and outcome signals. It does not imply AI emotion, consciousness, access to a person's true feelings, or an approved production architecture. Its mechanisms and measures remain unvalidated and outside the required EIP core.

## Technology boundary

EIP is technology-agnostic but evidence-demanding. Existing platforms should be retained when they can demonstrate the required context isolation, traceability, lifecycle control, human authority, change management, rights, and exit capabilities. A local model, open-weight model, gateway, retrieval system, agent platform, or middleware label does not by itself establish fitness, privacy, security, sovereignty, or compliance.

The Technical Assurance Layer and proposed Agent Control Profile are logical control models, not a mandatory product architecture. Their detailed fields, mappings, tests, and runtime specifications are not published here.

## Enterprise calibration

A bounded Enterprise Calibration allows an organization to configure and test EIP against one real workflow. It examines organizational, workflow, people, technical, evidence, rights, and commercial conditions. Where formation is investigated, the enterprise must identify the exact support being changed, keep other material conditions visible, and test transfer, escalation, total burden, and restoration. Negative evidence, review overload, unresolved dependencies, unfairness, and stop decisions are valid findings.

The calibration concludes with a recorded choice to scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop.

## Origin and transfer

EIP was developed from pharmaceutical R&D, Clinical Development, and Clinical Operations, where patient privacy, sponsor confidentiality, study boundaries, blinding, data integrity, safety, GxP expectations, and accountable human decisions create a high-stringency test environment.

The framework may be relevant beyond this domain, but transfer requires local re-examination rather than assumed equivalence.
