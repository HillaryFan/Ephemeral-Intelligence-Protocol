# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Purpose of this repository

This repository is a professional credibility and discussion entry point. It allows a reader to understand the problem EIP addresses, the governing principle, the five-protocol architecture, the evidence posture, and the next decision without receiving the implementation package.

Public visibility is not treated as a substitute for controlled review.

## Published here

- framework name, authorship, development position, and origin;
- the Default Ephemeral principle and deliberate persistence boundary;
- high-level definitions of the three information states;
- names and one-paragraph operating intent of the five protocols;
- the purpose and possible outcomes of a bounded Enterprise Calibration;
- high-level descriptions of the three-volume set and reader paths;
- explicit non-claims and evidence expectations;
- rights, attribution, and responsible-feedback notice.

## Deliberately withheld

- complete White Paper, Implementation Manual, Technical Companion, or Executive Package files;
- detailed workflows, stage gates, review thresholds, scoring logic, workload formulas, and decision templates;
- source code, pseudocode, data schemas, object models, APIs, prompts, evaluation sets, and machine-readable control profiles;
- component-level retention, cache, telemetry, backup, deletion, credential, network, tool, and agent-control specifications;
- validation protocols, test scripts, failure cases, incident procedures, rollback mechanics, and evidence records;
- contract clauses, pricing, incentive formulas, supplier negotiation positions, IP allocation mechanisms, and exit packages;
- detailed clinical cases, sponsor or study logic, quantitative outcomes, proprietary diagrams, and source working papers;
- v2.1 Change Register, candidate change material, unpublished research notes, and future-version decisions.

## Research-horizon boundary

Portable contribution credentials, professional passports, zero-knowledge proof, cross-platform contribution scoring, differentiated AI identity, autonomy budgets, and related workforce-governance ideas are not part of the v2.0 five-protocol core. They should not be represented as deployed EIP capabilities or current implementation commitments.

## Responsible discussion

Public Issues or Discussions, if enabled, should be used only for high-level questions about the framework. Do not submit confidential operational details, patient data, sponsor or study information, employer materials, unpublished inventions, security weaknesses, contract terms, or proprietary implementation proposals.

Substantive evaluation, implementation, licensing, collaboration, or research discussions should move to a controlled channel with an explicit purpose and disclosure boundary.

## Publication rule

If a detail would allow a reader to reconstruct a technical control, validation method, contract mechanism, scoring system, data lifecycle, or operational playbook, it does not belong in the public repository unless the author has separately approved that disclosure.
