# The EIP Three-Volume Set

The v2.0 Application Edition is organized as one operating model viewed through three leadership decisions. The full documents are maintained as controlled material and are not reproduced in this public repository.

## Volume I - Core White Paper

**Primary decision:** Does EIP merit a bounded organizational evaluation, and what must be true for the framework to create enterprise capability rather than additional AI activity?

The White Paper establishes the strategic thesis, Default Ephemeral principle, information states, organizational architecture, five protocols, decision rights, evidence logic, enterprise calibration, people model, technology boundary, and deliberate non-decisions.

## Volume II - Implementation Manual

**Primary decision:** How should one bounded workflow be configured, operated, measured, and closed so leadership can decide whether to scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop?

The Manual translates the framework into an enterprise-calibration sequence covering mandate, intended use, readiness, baseline, roles, workload protection, local protocol procedures, evidence collection, escalation, economics, and closure.

## Volume III - Technical, Governance & Contracting Companion

**Primary decision:** Can architecture, runtime controls, human authority, evidence, rights, contracts, change control, handover, and exit support the intended operating model?

The Companion converts policy and intended use into capability-first technical and contractual questions. It includes the logical Technical Assurance Layer and proposed Agent Control Profile while explicitly avoiding a mandatory product architecture.

## Reader paths

| Reader | Recommended controlled-review path | Decision enabled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CEO / Executive Committee | Volume I executive sections and evidence gates | Whether a bounded evaluation is justified and which outcomes matter. |
| CTO / CIO / CISO / Data and AI | Volume I technology boundary, then Volume III | Whether current architecture can evidence the required operating capabilities and where gaps remain. |
| Transformation Leader / AI Office | Volume I protocols and entry logic, then Volume II | How to configure, run, measure, and close the first bounded cycle. |
| Quality / Regulatory / Privacy / Legal / Procurement | Volume I decision rights, then Volume III | Which accountable decisions, evidence, rights, and controls must exist. |
| Business Owner / Frontline Expert / People Leader | Volume I people and workflow sections, then Volume II | What participation requires and what may become reusable capability. |

## Controlled review boundary

A fuller review should have a defined recipient, purpose, confidentiality expectation, and decision question. Sharing should be staged: public overview first, executive brief second, then selected or complete controlled volumes only when the discussion justifies the additional disclosure.

Candidate v2.1 changes are governed separately and do not alter the frozen v2.0 Application Edition unless formally approved and integrated.
