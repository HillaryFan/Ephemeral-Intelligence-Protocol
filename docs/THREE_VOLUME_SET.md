# The EIP Three-Volume Set

The v2.1 reviewed integration draft is organized as one operating model viewed through three leadership decisions. Version 2.0 remains a frozen historical baseline. The full documents are maintained as controlled material and are not reproduced in this public repository.

## Volume I - Core White Paper

**Primary decision:** Does EIP merit a bounded organizational evaluation, and what must be true for the framework to create enterprise capability rather than additional AI activity?

The White Paper establishes the strategic thesis, Default Ephemeral principle, information states, organizational architecture, five protocols, decision rights, evidence logic, enterprise calibration, people model, technology boundary, and deliberate non-decisions. Version 2.1 also develops the HITL operating range, Scaffold-to-Persistence formation inquiry, Outcome Efficiency, New Leadership, human-benefit allocation, and research boundaries.

## Volume II - Implementation Manual

**Primary decision:** How should one bounded workflow be configured, operated, measured, and closed so leadership can decide whether to scale, refine, remain bounded, pause, or stop?

The Manual translates the framework into an enterprise-calibration sequence covering mandate, intended use, readiness, baseline, roles, workload protection, local protocol procedures, evidence collection, escalation, economics, and closure. Version 2.1 adds support-state baselines, controlled withdrawal, transfer and recovery testing, review-capacity decisions, measurement definitions, and separate support-state and deployment-scope decisions.

## Volume III - Technical, Governance & Contracting Companion

**Primary decision:** Can architecture, runtime controls, human authority, evidence, rights, contracts, change control, handover, and exit support the intended operating model?

The Companion converts policy and intended use into capability-first technical and contractual questions. It includes the logical Technical Assurance Layer and proposed Agent Control Profile while explicitly avoiding a mandatory product architecture. Version 2.1 adds formation-memory governance, oversight routing and capacity limits, separate authority and competence records, withdrawal acceptance tests, and effects-based change classification.

## Reader paths

| Reader | Recommended controlled-review path | Decision enabled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CEO / Executive Committee | Volume I executive sections and evidence gates | Whether a bounded evaluation is justified, which outcomes matter, and where released capacity should go. |
| CTO / CIO / CISO / Data and AI | Volume I technology boundary, then Volume III | Whether current architecture can evidence the required operating capabilities and where gaps remain. |
| Transformation Leader / AI Office | Volume I protocols and entry logic, then Volume II | How to configure, run, measure, and close the first bounded cycle. |
| Quality / Regulatory / Privacy / Legal / Procurement | Volume I decision rights, then Volume III | Which accountable decisions, evidence, rights, and controls must exist. |
| Business Owner / Frontline Expert / People Leader | Volume I people and workflow sections, then Volume II | What participation requires, what may become reusable capability, and how workload, recovery, support, and benefit decisions are governed. |

## Controlled review boundary

A fuller review should have a defined recipient, purpose, confidentiality expectation, and decision question. Sharing should be staged: public overview first, executive brief second, then selected or complete controlled volumes only when the discussion justifies the additional disclosure.

The public repository provides a high-level v2.1 research baseline. Complete volumes, detailed measures, source registers, change records, candidate mechanisms, and implementation materials remain controlled.
