# Validation and Evidence Boundary

## Current development position

EIP v2.1 is an original, validation-ready enterprise AI operating-model framework prepared for bounded organizational pilot testing and refinement. Version 2.0 remains the frozen historical baseline; Version 2.1 is a reviewed integration draft, not a completed enterprise validation study.

In this context, **validation-ready** means that the framework defines a coherent problem, operating principle, roles, protocols, lifecycle, decision questions, evidence categories, and closure logic that an enterprise can configure and test. It does not mean that enterprise validation, regulatory acceptance, technical performance, economic benefit, or safe operation has already been demonstrated.

## What a bounded evaluation should establish

At a high level, an adopting enterprise should be able to evaluate:

- whether the intended use and prohibited uses are clear;
- whether Sponsor, study, role, blinding, data, jurisdiction, contract, and IP boundaries can be enforced;
- whether consequential human reviewers have context, time, competence, and real challenge authority;
- whether candidate knowledge can be minimized, validated, scoped, versioned, monitored, corrected, and withdrawn;
- whether technical and supplier claims are supported by component-level evidence;
- whether changes can trigger regression review, revalidation, rollback, suspension, or exit;
- whether rights, portability, provider substitution, and continuity are credible;
- whether benefits remain credible after review, governance, maintenance, and workload costs are counted.
- whether a claimed reduction in human cognitive support identifies the exact support changed and survives appropriate novelty, transfer, interruption, and recovery tests;
- whether qualified review capacity, queue limits, routing, timeout, restoration, and overload behavior preserve legitimate human authority;
- whether competence evidence and authority evidence remain separate, scoped, monitored, time-bounded, and reversible;
- whether released capacity becomes net reliable productive gain, human benefit, additional scope, recovery, learning, or downstream burden.

The public repository does not disclose the detailed evidence templates, thresholds, control mappings, test cases, or decision records used to answer these questions.

## Version 2.1 proposed evidence domains

Version 2.1 keeps four Outcome Efficiency domains visible rather than collapsing them into one score:

1. **Reliable productive outcomes** - useful results that satisfy the defined quality and intended-use boundary.
2. **Human cognitive sustainability** - the verification, correction, coordination, governance, and recovery burden required to sustain those results.
3. **Realized human benefit** - whether released capacity is deliberately returned to learning, deeper work, recovery, or another stated human objective.
4. **Governance integrity** - whether authority, evidence, escalation, monitoring, change, and stop conditions remain effective.

Candidate measures within these domains are proposed constructs that require local definition and testing. Their inclusion in the reviewed draft does not establish validated KPIs, psychometric properties, a universal deployment optimum, or a demonstrated supervision curve.

## Decision outcomes

A bounded evaluation should end with one of five explicit outcomes:

1. **Scale** - evidence supports broader use within a newly approved scope.
2. **Refine** - value signals exist, but control, quality, workload, evidence, or adoption design needs improvement.
3. **Remain bounded** - the capability is useful only within its current evidence boundary.
4. **Pause** - a material dependency or risk must be resolved before further operation.
5. **Stop** - evidence does not support continuation or an acceptable boundary cannot be maintained.

## Claims EIP does not make

EIP does not claim or guarantee:

- automatic GxP, privacy, security, legal, or regulatory compliance;
- secure deletion merely because information is labeled ephemeral;
- that all prompts, caches, logs, telemetry, backups, or records should be deleted;
- enterprise ROI, self-funding transformation, time-to-market gains, or headcount reduction;
- superior safety, decision quality, fairness, or reuse before evidence exists;
- that a 180-day period is a universal validation timeline;
- that any specific model, platform, supplier, architecture, or contract is fit for use;
- that contribution recognition is a portable credential or employee ranking system.
- that reduced prompting or fewer interventions proves reliable capability formation;
- that one formation topology, staffing model, support-withdrawal sequence, or HITL ratio is generally superior;
- that passing a competence test grants broader authority or removes mandatory human accountability;
- that experience-sensitive context modulation demonstrates AI feelings, consciousness, or better outcomes.

## Evidence before adjectives

Terms such as "secure," "private," "compliant," "sovereign," "validated," "agentic," and "local" are not treated as proof. EIP asks which component, configuration, contract, role, test, record, and accountable decision support the claim in the selected context.

This repository therefore uses restrained development language and omits unsupported performance claims.
